Our Mission

Donna Restaurant is a restaurant located in the heart of Baliyu. The chef has many years of experience and leads a team of chefs using innovation and superior technique. Donna offers a variety of gastronomic adventures with plenty of small and large plates, signature cocktails and a full sushi bar stocked with sashimi and nigiri options fresh from Japan.
